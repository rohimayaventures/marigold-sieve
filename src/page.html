Creative compliance agent

Ship creative. Safely.

Draft Meta ad copy and screen it against 9 health-advertising rules before it ships, with a human in the loop whenever the model is unsure.

  • 9 compliance rules checked
  • 2 models, routed by confidence
  • ? Uncertain? A human decides.
  1. 1

    Topic

    Start from a science topic, or paste a finished script.

  2. 2

    Draft

    A fast model writes a 30-second script, 3 hooks, and a caption.

  3. 3

    Review

    The script is screened against 9 rules and returned as JSON.

  4. 4

    Decide

    Pass, fix with a compliant rewrite, or escalate to a person.

  5. 5

    Route and log

    Low confidence goes to a stronger model. Escalations become a Zendesk-format ticket. Every run is logged with cost.
